**To:** Editorial Office, *Acta Medica Philippina*
**Subject:** Pre-submission inquiry: original computational study on *S. japonicum* infection and colorectal cancer

Dear Editor,

I am preparing an original research article titled "Cross-Species Transcriptomic Analysis Identifies Colorectal Cancer-Associated Molecular Pathways Induced by *Schistosoma japonicum* Infection." The study integrates publicly available RNA-seq data from *S. japonicum*-infected mouse colon with human colorectal adenocarcinoma data from The Cancer Genome Atlas, under a pre-specified analysis plan, to identify shared inflammatory, oxidative-stress and DNA-damage-response pathways. It generates new analyses and results but uses no newly collected patient samples or animals.

Would an original cross-species computational transcriptomic study of this kind, using public experimental and TCGA datasets, fall within the journal's scope for human-health research?

Thank you for your guidance.
