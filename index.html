  • Highest possible score: 60 points. Each question is read twice.
  • Wait for the “Go” signal, write on the board, and raise it when time is up.
  • Easy round: solve in your head. No writing on the table, palm, or in the air.
  • Write answers in lowest terms and always include units (cm², km/h).
  • Watch for “not” and “except” in the question. Spell science words carefully.
